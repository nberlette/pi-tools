# Raspberry Pi 4 Model B (4B)

The Raspberry Pi 4 Model B is a versatile and powerful single-board computer that serves as the fourth generation of the popular Raspberry Pi series. Released in June 2019, it offers significant improvements in performance, connectivity, and memory options compared to its predecessors, making it suitable for a wide range of applications from education to professional projects.

With its quad-core ARM Cortex-A72 CPU, up to 8GB of RAM, dual-display support, and enhanced networking capabilities, the Raspberry Pi 4B is designed to meet the needs of developers, hobbyists, and educators alike.
